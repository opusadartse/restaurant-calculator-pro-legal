1. What this app does

Restaurant Calculator Pro is a restaurant calculator for iPhone and iPad. It helps you split a check across cards, calculate a tip, apply a general percentage, use calculator memory, and review calculations from the current session.

2. No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking

Restaurant Calculator Pro does not create accounts, does not require a sign-in, and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software. It does not use App Tracking Transparency tracking and does not sell personal data.

3. Calculations stay on your device

Check amounts, tips, percentages, the calculation tape, calculator memory, and HISTORY are processed on your device for the current session only. HISTORY is not saved after you close the app. Adartse Corporation does not collect calculator entries.

4. No cloud, database, or network collection

The audited Version 1.0 implementation does not include a user database, cloud synchronization, or networking used to send your calculations or other user data to Adartse Corporation or to a third party.

5. Permissions

The app does not request access to Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone, Location, Health, or Bluetooth for its calculator functions.

6. Children

Restaurant Calculator Pro is a general utility. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
