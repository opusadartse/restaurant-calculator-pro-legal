A professional restaurant calculator

Restaurant Calculator Pro is designed for fast restaurant calculations on iPhone and iPad. Split a check to the last cent, calculate tips, run general percentages, and review the current session on a clean professional keypad.

Features

  • Fast check splitting with exact cent allocation
  • Remaining cents assigned to the final card
  • Tip calculations and custom tip percentages
  • General percentage calculator
  • Calculation tape, session HISTORY, and calculator memory
  • iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape

Calculations stay on your device for the current session. No account is required.
